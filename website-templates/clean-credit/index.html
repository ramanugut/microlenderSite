Questions and answers

Things worth knowing.

Short answers to the questions customers usually need before applying.

Does applying mean I am approved?

No. Approval depends on the required affordability, identity, credit and other applicable checks.

Will I see the repayment before I accept?

Yes. The applicable repayment information should be shown before you accept an approved loan.

What documents will I need?

The application journey will show the supporting documents needed for your assessment.

Can I come back to my application?

Your account is designed to keep your application and loan information together so you can continue your journey.
