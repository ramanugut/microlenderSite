Guides

Useful things to know before borrowing.

Short, practical information about affordability, loan costs and staying safe online.

Affordability

Why your expenses matter

Income alone does not show what you can afford. Normal household costs and existing repayments matter too.

Costs

Check the total, not only the cash

Look at the amount borrowed, interest, fees, repayment date and total amount you will repay.

Safety

Keep your banking credentials private

Never share your banking PIN, banking password or a banking OTP inside a loan application form.
