Code of Practice

The principles used to keep the lending journey clear, fair and responsible.

Code of PracticePrivacyTerms of Use

1. Treat customers fairly

Customers should receive clear information, reasonable support and respectful treatment throughout the credit journey. The interface should not pressure customers to borrow more than they need or hide important information.

2. Lend responsibly

Credit should only be considered after the required affordability and credit checks. An application does not guarantee approval.

3. Show the full cost

Before an approved loan is accepted, the customer should be able to see the loan amount, interest, fees, repayment dates and total repayment.

4. Protect personal information

Collect only information needed for a lawful purpose and use appropriate safeguards to protect customer information and documents.

5. Support customers in difficulty

Customers who are struggling with repayments should be treated professionally and given clear information about the available account support process.

6. Handle complaints properly

Complaints should be recorded, investigated and answered through the lender's approved complaints process.

7. Keep reliable records

Applications, decisions, documents, payments and servicing actions should be recorded accurately so the account history can be reviewed when needed.
