Privacy

How information is used when you apply for credit or use your account.

Code of PracticePrivacyTerms of Use

1. Information used

Information may include your identity and contact details, employment and income information, monthly expenses, banking details, supporting documents, application history and account activity.

2. Why it is used

Information is used to process applications, assess affordability and eligibility, manage customer accounts, prevent misuse, communicate with customers and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Service providers

Information may be handled by service providers that support hosting, identity, communications, payments, verification, document processing or other functions needed to provide the service. Access should be limited to what is required for that purpose.

4. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect customer information and uploaded documents from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

5. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as it is reasonably required for the credit relationship, legal obligations, disputes, fraud prevention and legitimate operational needs.

6. Your information

You can update supported profile information from My details in your customer account. Other privacy or information requests should be handled through the lender's customer support process.

7. Website data

Security and session information may be used to keep the website and customer account working safely. This notice should be updated if the way information is collected or used materially changes.
