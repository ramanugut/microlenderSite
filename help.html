What do you need help with?

Quick answers about your application, documents and repayments.

Common questions

Check my account
What happens after I apply?

Your application is reviewed. You can see its current status in your account.

What documents do I need?

The application shows the documents needed for your application. If an existing document can be reused, you will not be asked to upload it again.

Why do you ask about my expenses?

Income and normal monthly expenses are used for the affordability assessment.

Can I change my personal details?

Yes. Log in and open My details to update your saved contact and residential information.

Where do I see my application or loan?

Log in to your account. Applications and loans are kept in separate tabs so you can find them quickly.

Where will my loan documents appear?

Documents linked to an active or previous loan are shown inside that loan in your account.
